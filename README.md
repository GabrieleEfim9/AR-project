# AR-project | Gabriele Efim (s361144)
The advent of Industry 4.0 has exponentially increased the number of IoT devices and connected sensors within manufacturing environments. While data collection is highly automated, the on-site retrieval of this data by human operators may often require the navigation through complex dashboards or interacting with physical interfaces on the machines themselves.
The proposal of this paper is a marker-based Web Augmented Reality (WebAR) architecture in order to bridge the gap between physical machinery and digital diagnostics. Operators can instantly visualize real-time telemetry rendered as a 3D holographic panel anchored to the physical environment by simply aiming a smartphone camera at a matrix barcode attached to the device. The core advantage of this solution is its zero-install nature: leveraging WebGL and WebRTC, the entire computer vision and rendering pipeline runs directly within the mobile browser.
